# CHIROMANTIA

[Română](README.ro.md) · **English** · [Français](README.fr.md) · [Italiano](README.it.md) · [Español](README.es.md) · [Português](README.pt.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Magyar](README.hu.md)

*An interactive palmistry atlas in a single offline HTML file.*

CHIROMANTIA is an interactive palm-reading atlas in the tradition of palmistry: an SVG map of the palm with left and right hand, lines, mounts, fingers, hand types, fingerprints and 2D:4D measurements, complemented by a science and critical-thinking section. Everything lives in one HTML file, with no account, no trackers and no network. It comes from the "trade-free" philosophy.

## Features

- SVG palm map, left or right hand, with the current selection highlighted and spoken aloud
- Lines, mounts, fingers and nails, hand types, signs, fingerprints and creases — each claim tagged with its level of evidence
- Guided two-hand reading, saved profiles, comparison, shareable image, link and QR code (no server)
- 2D:4D and proportion measurements with Monte-Carlo uncertainty simulation and a logged chart
- Photo measurement calibrated with a card (85.6 mm), plus an alignment template and annotations
- Printable 1:1 worksheet
- Procedural fingerprint trainer, Leitner flashcards, map game, quiz, search
- Critical thinking: Barnum blind test, cold-reading simulator, testable predictions with due dates, science and history
- Listen to the map: lines turned into sound
- 8 languages (ro, en, fr, it, es, pt, de, hu); one HTML file; works offline

## How to use

Open the HTML file in a modern browser (double-click). Pick a language, then explore the hand map, the entries and the guided reading. No installation, account or internet connection is needed.

## Privacy

The app sends nothing anywhere and contains no trackers or analytics. Preferences (language, theme, progress) are stored only in localStorage in your own browser; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings. Any photos stay local.

## Limits and disclaimer

Palmistry is cultural entertainment and a historical tradition; it is not scientifically validated. The content is not medical, legal or psychological advice and does not predict the future. Do not use the app for important decisions. Ask for consent from the person whose hand you read.

## Accessibility

Full keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, high contrast and respect for the reduced-motion preference.

## Offline install / PWA

Save the file and open it any time, without a network. If you host it over HTTPS, your browser may offer "Install app" / "Add to home screen".

## Build from source

Sources are in src/ (data in src/data/*.json). Run python3 build.py; the output appears in dist/. Validate with python3 validate.py.
